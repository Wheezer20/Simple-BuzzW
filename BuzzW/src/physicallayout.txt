Buzzer: Connect to a digital pin (e.g., D3) and GND.
Wire Course: One end to GND, the other to a digital input (e.g., D2) with pull-up enabled.
Loop (Wand): Connect to GND or another digital pin.
Buttons: Use the shield’s left and right buttons for start/stop (check your shield’s pinout).
Display: Already handled by the shield library.

Tip: Use male-to-male wires to connect the course and wand to the shield’s digital pins and GND.
